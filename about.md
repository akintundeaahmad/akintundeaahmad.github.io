My career spans enterprise technology, entrepreneurial development, hospitality, sustainable fashion, and documentary storytelling.

I lead marketing and communications for ESO Ventures, co-founded Oakland’s coffee shop and wine bar Kinfolx, and founded Ade Dehye, a fashion brand rooted in West African craftsmanship and sustainable production.

Before business school, my journalism and documentary work explored education, economic inequality, justice, and representation. That foundation continues to shape how I work: understanding people, asking thoughtful questions, and translating complex ideas into stories that resonate.

Across my projects, I combine creative judgment with business strategy to help organizations communicate their value, reach audiences, and build lasting relationships.

My business interests include product marketing, entrepreneurship, venture capital, and business strategy.
