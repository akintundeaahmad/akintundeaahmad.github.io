## ESO Ventures
{: #eso-ventures}

**Senior Marketing & Communications Manager**  
February 2023–present · Transitioned to a contractor role in August 2025

ESO Ventures builds entrepreneurial infrastructure for underinvested founders, particularly Black, Latino, and system-impacted entrepreneurs. Its model combines business incubators, access to capital, business support services, and an ongoing entrepreneurial community.

I lead marketing and communications that connect ESO’s work with entrepreneurs, government partners, funders, and the broader public. My responsibilities include brand positioning, website development, email marketing, social media, entrepreneur profiles, and partnership storytelling.

Selected contributions:

- Led a website rebuild and strengthened ESO’s digital presence.
- Designed a CRM-driven lead funnel for more than 600 entrepreneurs, increasing incubator applications by 20%.
- Led go-to-market communications for the Build Where You Are initiative, supporting contracts with Antioch, Oakland, and Santa Clara.
- Created newsletters, social content, and impact stories for entrepreneur and institutional audiences.
- Developed partner interview and video concepts to communicate the value of local investment in entrepreneurship.

## Kinfolx
{: #kinfolx}

**Co-Founder**  
Oakland, California · Opened in 2022

Kinfolx is a coffee shop, wine bar, and community gathering space in Uptown Oakland. We created it as a place where hospitality, culture, and connection come together.

The business has generated more than $1 million in annual revenue while serving as a gathering place for residents, artists, entrepreneurs, and community organizations.

My work includes brand building, community partnerships, event programming, promotion, and operational coordination. Through music, art, conversations, and cultural events, I help turn the space into an active part of Oakland’s creative and entrepreneurial community.

Kinfolx reflects my belief that a business can be commercially successful while contributing meaningfully to the place it calls home.

## Ade Dehye
{: #ade-dehye}

**Founder & CEO**  
Founded February 2021 · Production in Accra, Ghana

Ade Dehye is a fashion brand rooted in West African craftsmanship, cultural expression, and sustainable production.

I built the business across brand strategy, product development, production, e-commerce, marketing, and partnerships. The brand incorporates handwoven and hand-dyed West African textiles alongside upcycled materials sourced from Ghana’s Kantamanto market.

Selected highlights:

- Generated approximately $250,000 in cumulative revenue as of August 2026.
- Built production operations in Accra involving 15 sewing operators.
- Developed collaborations with Stephen Curry, the National Basketball Players Association, and Intuit.
- Connected traditional textile practices with contemporary fashion and international customers.

The venture has given me practical experience bringing products to market, managing production, developing a differentiated brand, and balancing commercial goals with social and environmental commitments.
